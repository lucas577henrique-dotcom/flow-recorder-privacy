# Flow Recorder — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 19 October 2026  
**Applies to:** Flow Recorder browser extension, version 45.0.5.4

## 1. Purpose
Flow Recorder helps users document browser workflows by recording user-initiated interactions, screenshots, navigation and process steps. The extension is designed for process documentation, analysis and automation development.

## 2. Data the extension can process
Depending on the pages that users choose to record, the extension may process:
- Browser activity: page URLs, navigation events, interaction timestamps, clicks and other recorded activities.
- Web content: labels, text, screenshots, images and visual page elements.
- Workflow information: project names, process and step descriptions, annotations, review states and exported documents.
- Technical preferences: local application settings, saved recording sessions and local reporting information.
- Sensitive content: information visible on recorded websites may include names, email addresses, messages or other personal, financial or confidential data.

The extension is **not intended** to collect passwords, authentication tokens, payment details or other secrets. Certain fields and URLs are subject to masking or sanitization, but these protections cannot guarantee that all sensitive material is removed.

## 3. Use of data
Data is used to enable users to capture activities, review and organize recorded steps, create documentation, run local analyses, and import or export workflow packages.

## 4. Storage and sharing
The reviewed release primarily stores data and settings locally in browser/device storage. No developer-operated cloud synchronization service was identified in the reviewed package. Users may export data to local files and decide whether and with whom to share those files.

The developer does not intentionally sell recorded user data or transmit recordings to an advertising service. Websites users choose to visit or record may independently process data under their own policies.

## 5. Retention, access and deletion
Recorded projects and preferences remain in local storage until the user removes them or clears browser/extension storage, subject to browser behavior. Exported files are separately controlled by the user, who must delete them wherever they are saved or shared.

## 6. Permissions
The extension requests browser permissions including scripting, storage, activeTab, unlimitedStorage, tabs, downloads, webNavigation, and optional website access. These are used for user-authorized recording, navigation awareness, local persistence and exports.

## 7. Security and responsibilities
Users must obtain authorization before recording websites, personal information or confidential material. They should review and redact recordings and exports before sharing them, and refrain from capturing credentials or sensitive personal data.

## 8. Changes
This policy may be updated as the extension changes. The revision date above will be updated accordingly.

## 9. Contact and privacy requests

For questions about this policy, contact the developer via the [public Flow Recorder privacy repository's Issues page](https://github.com/lucas577henrique-dotcom/flow-recorder-privacy/issues). Do not post personal, confidential, or sensitive information in public issues. For confidential enquiries, request a non-public contact channel without including personal data in the issue.
